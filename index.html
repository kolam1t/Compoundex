Country & Geopolitical Risk

What this model predicts

Compoundex teaches students to value real companies with full discounted cash flow (DCF) models. Every DCF needs a discount rate, and for any company with material operations abroad that rate must include a country risk premium.

This model predicts the probability that a country will experience elevated geopolitical risk over the next 12 months — sovereign stress, conflict escalation, a major sanctions action or a disorderly political transition. When a student values a company exposed to a flagged country, the platform prompts them to widen the country risk premium, and the same signal is used to review the geographic exposure of the live model portfolio.
